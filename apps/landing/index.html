Questions

Good to know

How much does Ahia cost?

Joining, browsing and buying are free. Sellers pay a small commission only when a sale completes, and riders pay a commission on completed deliveries. Optional extras such as featuring your shop or a Business plan are priced up front before you pay.

How does paying work?

Add money to your Ahia wallet by card or bank transfer, then pay for an order once the seller has accepted it. The seller is credited after you collect the goods with your private code, so your money is never sent before the order happens.

What if the seller doesn’t have my item?

A seller has to accept your request first. Once they do, the stock is reserved for you for a week. If an order is cancelled before it’s completed, what you paid goes back to your wallet.

Who are the riders?

Riders register in the app and upload their profile photo, licence, vehicle papers and ID for our team to check. You choose a rider near you, see their vehicle and plate number, and they can only complete the delivery with your code.

What does the Verified badge mean?

It means Ahia has checked the business’s identity, contact details and location. It can’t be bought, and it isn’t a promise that every product is perfect — that’s what reviews and our support team are for.

Where is Ahia available?

Ahia works across Nigeria, starting in Lagos. Open it in any web browser, or download the Android app (an early test version installed straight from this site, so your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser). The iPhone app is on its way.
