# Updated marketplace brief implementation review

The supplied updated local brief adds Local Status (section 4.16) and extends advertising, notification and release acceptance requirements. This implementation delivers Local Status with photos, short videos, Boost campaigns and booking attribution in the existing Express API, React Native app and operations console. It keeps the four-item mobile navigation and existing purchase, wallet, rider and shopping flows.

## Subsequent video campaigns and conversion implementation

The follow-up batch implements short videos, independent Boost Status campaigns and booking conversion tracking. [Media campaigns and attribution](status-media-campaigns.md) documents the delivered limits and accounting rules. The table above reflects the combined implementation.

- Videos have real duration/container validation, compression, poster generation and tap-to-play playback.
- Boost campaigns have their own held budget, duration, state/LGA or state/city targets, staff review and unused-budget refunds. Paid reach is labelled and measured separately.
- Click references bind buyer and product for 24-hour single-use booking attribution. Dashboards report booking/completion counts and completed gross value, with no promise of causal attribution or guaranteed reach.
- Remaining later work includes direct enquiry attribution, category digests, richer ranking, adaptive streaming and physical-device/production playback acceptance.

## Existing broader brief gaps

These are not newly delivered by the Local Status module: complete country/street/block/stall address fields and market walkway mapping; granular driver identity/vehicle/permit/insurance badges; category/prohibited-goods policy administration; archive/stale-inventory expiry controls; privacy-preserving voice relay; driver ratings; richer delivery milestones/returns scheduling; continuous background GPS; device/store/provider acceptance; and business/legal launch sign-off. Existing dispute, evidence, ledger, pickup/delivery and staff tools remain available.

Session decisions take precedence over generic directions in the document: OTP remains **email through Nodemailer**, the normal reservation remains **one week**, marketplace sales commission remains **5%**, and app funds remain in development simulation until the agreed provider checks. No deployment or automated test suite was requested for this batch.

## Verification

The local migrations were applied. A fictional-account walkthrough confirmed publishing, nearby and same-LGA visibility, exclusion in another LGA, private-field omission, deduplicated aggregate analytics, seller muting, report counts, expiry, admin removal, an unchanged linked listing, and no publication broadcast. Typechecks, API/admin builds, the mobile web export and API contract consistency pass. The mobile UI also completed area selection, story viewing, product navigation, photo preview and publication. A rolled-back notification walkthrough confirmed zero new-product alerts while opted out and one alert after two publications while opted in. A further fictional-account walkthrough verified video processing, invalid-file rejection, targeted paid discovery, pending and prorated active refunds, and booking attribution through pickup completion. Automated tests remain deferred. Physical-device playback/GPS and production S3 video delivery still need acceptance checks.
